# Amity
## Little Bit About Me
Hallo! My name is Amity. I'm a creator of sorts, a creator of many things. You'd be hard-pressed to find anything I haven't even dabbled in.

My interests lie among entertainment. Video games, films, music, theatre, books. I enjoy creating entertaining things that are either novel just for their existence, or of the story they tell.
I've made video games (which is why I'm here on Github in the first place), songs, films/videos, scripts, and enough writing to drown a human in ink.

My biggest project so far is a world-building project, which I pour all my heart into. It touches everything I love doing. I create stories, games, songs, and notably, conlangs.
I like to call my world-building project, "Project Krëshkia"! Krëshkia being the name of the continent where most of the cool shit happens. It's home to my fictional races, the most unique (and my favourite) being the plezdrakons.
I've even got a conlang brewing up for them, B).

There's something I almost completely forgot to mention, I'm also a huge fan of languages. And when I say I'm a huge fan, I mean EIGHT DUOLINGO COURSES, THREE YEARS OF WORLD LANGUAGES, and PLANS TO CREATE MULTIPLE LANGUAGES FROM SCRATCH FOR MY WORLD-BUILDING PROJECT!!!
My mother-tongue is American English though, but I've been studying German for a while and I can sometimes pick up on conversations other people are having when they think I don't know German. For eight duolingo courses, I'm not very far in most of them. My top three courses are German, Russian, and French. I don't like French that much. I've got like 1000xp in French, 8000xp in Russian, and almost 23000xp in German (as of writing this). Native children of those languages can out compete me in any literary game in their language.

## Q&A
**Other than concepts, what *things* am I interested in?**   
Lemme give you a list of things I like in no particular order: Minecraft, Legend of Zelda (specifically Ocarina of Time, Majora's Mask, Breath of the Wild, Tears of the Kingdom, Twilight Princess, and Skyward Sword), The Owl House, Gravity Falls, Wings of Fire, Undertale, Iron Giant, Slay the Spire, Detroit: Become Human, Dungeons & Dragons, Legos, Libraries, VR, Toki Pona, and probably lots n' lots more.

**What's your gender/pronouns?**   
I'm non-binary and my pronouns are they/them, but I don't mind she/her pronouns.

**What's your dream career?**   
My dream career is to get a job on the art side (but not too far from the tech side) of video game design. Simply put, I want to be a game designer. I'm not sure if I'd want to be part of a big team like in a commercial game company, or if I'd like to be part of a smaller group, if in a group at all. I haven't really gotten to work with others on a large project before, so everything I have made had to be small to stay manageable. But yeah, I'd really much like to make money off of game design & development. As long as I get to progress my dream project, Project Krëshkia! Oh yeah!

**What's your dream project**   
My dream project, is probably Project Krëshkia. One of my goals is to make a huge game about Krëshkia, much like Legend of Zelda. I want to bring Krëshkia to life basically (and kinda start a fandom/community around something I made, but that might just be respect-seeking side of me). Another goal is to write a book or series of books about Krëshkia, and maybe even make some short films, TV series, or full length films. It's a lot, and something like that only really comes after decades and decades of work, and historically the singular person behind such a thing ends up dying well before their project actually becomes popular. My best hope is to make something like Undertale. A semi-small-largish thing, that quickly makes an undying community full of determination.   
So I guess that's my dream project, to make something that someone loves.
